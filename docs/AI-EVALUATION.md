## Inspect the review decisions

1. **[PowerPortals film and critique](https://github.com/RawPowered/power-portals-ai-video-presentation-showcase)** — a finished 1:54 film with 17 studies connecting feedback, authentic frames, revisions, and trade-offs. Joseph owned audience/message choices, critique, and final creative direction. AI assisted production and documentation. Critique overlays are retrospective.
2. **[LuxuryCatalog design process](https://github.com/RawPowered/LuxuryCatalog/blob/main/docs/DESIGN-PROCESS.md)** — supplied line art, material instructions, geometry and finish review, variants, and catalog publishing. Preserved inputs and outputs support comparison; they do not establish manufacturing dimensions.
3. **[Earth To Jay rejection study](https://github.com/RawPowered/Earth-To-Jay-Portfolio/blob/main/design-critique/README.md)** — character fidelity, silhouette, anatomy, style, and story-fit failures turned into corrective direction. Rejected development images are labeled explicitly.
