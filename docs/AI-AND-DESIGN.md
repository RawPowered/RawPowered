## Review the decisions

For a short review, choose one visual case and one interactive or systems case. Follow the brief, inspect the artifact, read the critique, then compare the proposed correction with the evidence available. The work supports discussion of design judgment, implementation, and quality review without implying employment as an AI tutor or foundation-model training experience.

For systems and automation roles, continue to [API and automation cases](https://github.com/RawPowered/API-Integration-Automation-Portfolio), [systems administration](https://github.com/RawPowered/Systems-Administration-Portfolio), and [engineering decisions](ENGINEERING.md).
