# Why this portfolio is new

**These public projects were published recently. The work behind them spans roughly 15 years.**

I worked inside operating companies and alongside their leaders, with hands-on responsibility for systems supporting sales, service, fulfillment, and growth. I concentrated on improving operations. Code, CRM data, workflows, customer records, and operating details stayed in each company's systems. Much of that work was private by design and remained part of the company's internal history.

Some employers closed, platforms aged, and systems moved to new tools. The records that survive are incomplete. This portfolio brings together public-safe demonstrations and carefully scoped case studies so recruiters can review my decisions, interfaces, integrations, and automation. I label partial history, separate reported outcomes from evidence still available for review, and keep private company and customer details out. I label fictional data in demonstrations.

## How I work

I have spent much of my career as a hands-on, entrepreneurial operator working closely with business leaders. I learn the process, identify the constraint, then build or improve the system and carry it into operation. Working closely with operators helped me make decisions quickly and keep them grounded in actual workflows.

Some of my work predates commercially available LLM assistants. I use Cursor and Codex as development tools today. I set requirements and architecture, review source, debug failures, and define what is ready to release.
