## Experience behind the software

At Expert Solar, I administered CRM and business systems, took over outstanding internal development requests, and supported the Salesforce-to-Zoho transition. Taking over delivery enabled the company to discontinue a development contract, reducing recurring external development expense by approximately **$20K/month**.

[![Preserved partial history: 35K+ retained entries, 2,997 deal records and 13 surviving workflow definitions](assets/profile/operations-evidence.svg)](docs/EXPERT-SOLAR.md)

The legacy account preserves partial history of a previously functional system. Its retained data, configuration, and creation/modification metadata provide concrete supporting evidence; the complete historical automation stack is no longer available to demonstrate. The expense reduction is my reported business outcome, separate from the CRM observations.
